Frequently Asked Questions

What documents are required for Rentora car subscription? expand_more

You only need a valid original Indian Driving License (min. 1 year old) and Aadhaar card or Passport for identity and address verification. For registered businesses, GSTIN and PAN are required for tax invoicing.

Is zero-depreciation insurance included? expand_more

Yes. Every Rentora subscription vehicle comes with commercial zero-dep insurance. In the rare event of damage, maximum customer liability is capped at ₹9,999.

Can I extend my tenure or return the car early? expand_more

Absolutely. You can extend your subscription at any time directly through your Rentora dashboard. If you need to return early, simply give a 15-day notice with zero hidden cancellation penalties.

Who takes care of vehicle maintenance and routine servicing? expand_more

Rentora takes care of 100% of periodic servicing, engine oil changes, brake pads, and wear-and-tear items. Our authorized technicians pick up and drop off the vehicle from your doorstep at zero cost to you.
