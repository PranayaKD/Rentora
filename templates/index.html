Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about renting a car with Rentora in Odisha

What documents are required to book and drive? expand_more

You need an original Indian Driving License (valid for at least 1 year) and an Aadhaar Card or Passport. Verification takes 60 seconds via our digital DigiLocker integration. Zero physical photocopies needed.

How does the ₹0 security deposit work? expand_more

Verified users with completed DigiLocker KYC qualify for ₹0 security deposit on hatchbacks, sedans, and standard SUVs. No credit card block is placed. You pay only for your rental duration.

How do I unlock the vehicle without a physical key? expand_more

Once your booking starts and you complete the 2-minute digital photo inspection on your phone, an "Unlock" button appears in your dashboard. It communicates via secure encrypted Bluetooth directly with the vehicle's onboard telematics.

Can I pick up at Bhubaneswar Airport and drop off in Puri? expand_more

Yes. We support inter-hub drop-offs between Bhubaneswar, Cuttack, and Puri. Simply select your chosen drop-off hub when configuring your reservation.

How are highway toll charges handled? expand_more

All Rentora vehicles are equipped with an active commercial FASTag. When you cross NH-16 or state highway toll plazas, the gates open automatically. The exact toll amounts are settled transparently at trip checkout without extra fees.
