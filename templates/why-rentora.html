Frequently Asked Questions

How does keyless unlocking work? expand_more

Once your booking starts, an "Unlock Car" button activates on your Rentora booking dashboard. Simply tap it, and via secure Bluetooth connection, your car unlocks without needing a physical key exchange.

What documents do I need to book? expand_more

You only need an original valid Indian Driving License (held for at least 1 year) and an Aadhaar Card or Passport. Complete 60-second DigiLocker verification right on your phone.

What happens in case of a roadside breakdown? expand_more

Rentora provides 24/7 roadside assistance across Odisha. Call our emergency hotline or tap SOS in your dashboard, and we dispatch on-site technical support or a replacement vehicle immediately.
