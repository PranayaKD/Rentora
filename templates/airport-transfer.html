Airport Transfer Questions

What happens if my flight is delayed? expand_more

When you enter your flight number, our system tracks flight radar in real-time. If your flight is delayed by 30 minutes or 3 hours, our driver and vehicle schedule updates automatically with 60 minutes of complimentary waiting time after touchdown.

Are airport toll charges and parking vouchers included? expand_more

Yes! All Rentora airport bookings are 100% all-inclusive. Airport parking entry fees and expressway toll tickets are included in your quote, so you never have to pay extra cash upon exit.

How do I find my self-drive car or chauffeur? expand_more

For Chauffeur Meet & Greet, your driver stands right at the Arrivals Gate holding a Rentora placard with your name. For Terminal Self-Drive, you receive the exact parking bay number and keyless Bluetooth unlock pin via SMS/WhatsApp as soon as you land.
