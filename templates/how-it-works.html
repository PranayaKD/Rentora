Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

What documents are required to rent a car? expand_more

You only need an original valid Driving Licence (minimum 1 year old) and Aadhaar or Passport verification via DigiLocker during OTP signup. Zero physical copies needed.

How does the fuel policy work? expand_more

We follow a transparent same-to-same fuel policy. The fuel level at the start of your booking will be documented digitally on the app checklist, and you simply return the vehicle at the same level.

Is a security deposit mandatory? expand_more

Rentora offers ₹0 Security Deposit options on verified profiles and standard vehicles. For select premium cars, deposits are refunded instantly within 24 hours of return.
